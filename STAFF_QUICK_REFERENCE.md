## 🔍 HOW TO HELP CUSTOMERS (3 STEPS)

### **Customer asks: "Where is [product]?"**

#### **STEP 1:** Open Navigation
- Go to Navigation page
- You're at "Cashier Counter"

#### **STEP 2:** Search Product
- Type product name
- Click from suggestions

#### **STEP 3:** Tell Shelf Number
- Read the BIG number shown
- Say: "At Shelf #X"
